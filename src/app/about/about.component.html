About Us

It all started with a dream — a single Indigenous woman named Terri Haineault who refused to let her circumstances define her future.

As a determined mother and entrepreneur, Terri turned challenges into motivation and built her business from the ground up with courage, resilience, and heart. What began as a simple idea grew through long days, hard work, and an unshakable belief in creating something better — not just for herself, but for her family and community.

Every item we lift and every job we complete is powered by her story — a story of strength, perseverance, and pride. We don't just move and remove; we carry forward a vision built on independence, integrity, and the drive to succeed against all odds.
