Junk Removal Pricing

We price based on size

Got Just One Item?

We offer special pricing starting from $50 for single-item pickups! The cost depends on the size and type of the item, and where it's located (e.g., basement, main floor, 2nd floor, elevator available or not).

For instance, hauling away a microwave is cheaper than removing a large dresser.

Examples of items eligible for single-item pricing:
  • Small kitchen gadgets (microwaves, toaster ovens)
  • Monitors or desktop computers
  • Mattresses of any size
  • Large appliances (washers, dryers, BBQs)
  • Furniture (sofas, bed frames, dining tables)
  • TVs (any make or model)
  • And many more…

Got More Than One Item? We Price by Volume

If you're clearing out multiple items, pricing is based on how much space your items take up in the truck. The more space used, the higher the price tier.

We have a minimum charge for a quarter truckload, and pricing increases based on how full the truck gets. We confirm the exact cost on-site — no surprises.

Our Standard Volume Rates:
  • ¼ Truckload — starting at $150
  • ½ Truckload — $300
  • Full Truckload — $600

We're happy to give you a clear estimate when we arrive on-site. You’ll always know the price before we begin hauling.
